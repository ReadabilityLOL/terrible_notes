## Sets

[[Sets]] are <u>groups of stuff</u>
> Ex. $\{2,4,6\}$, Odd integers, black sheep...

The normal way to construct a set is like this:

$\{x|\text{x is a real number}\}$

If something is in a set, we denote it as such:

$$x \in \{...\}$$

If something's not in a set, we similarly denote it:

$$x \notin \{...\}$$

Two sets are equal if every member in the first one is also in the second one, and vice versa.

Two sets have a one-to-one correspondence if they have the same length, so that every element of $A$ can be paired with one element of $B$.
$$
A = \set {1,2,3}\\
B = \set {a,b,c}
$$
can be paired as 
$$
(1,a), (2,b), (3,c)
$$
or as 
$$
(1,b),(2,c),(3,a)
$$
among other things.

The [[Cartesian Product]] of two sets, $A$ and $B$ is the set of all [[ordered pairs]] of elements, denoted $(x,y)$, where $x \in A$ and $y \in B$. 
This is written as $A \times B$.  
Ex:
$\set {1,2} \times \set {a,b} = (1,a), (1,b), (2,a), (2,b)$
and  
$\set {a,b} \times \set {1,2}= (a,1), (a,2), (b,1), (b,2)$
